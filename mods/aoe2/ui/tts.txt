; Civilization bonuses (spoken in the faction picker via G; indented lines continue)
8520 Unique unit Longbowman.
  Unique techs Yeomen and Warwolf.
  Foot archers plus one range in Castle Age and plus one more in Imperial Age, except skirmishers.
  Shepherds work faster.
  Town Centers cost half wood from Castle Age.
  Team bonus: Archery Ranges work faster.
  No Bloodlines, no Thumb Ring.
8521 Unique unit Throwing Axeman.
  Unique techs Bearded Axe and Chivalry.
  Cavalry plus twenty percent hit points from Feudal Age.
  Foragers work faster.
  Farm upgrades are free.
  Castles cost less stone.
  Team bonus: Knights plus two line of sight.
  No Thumb Ring, Bracer, Ring Archer Armor, or Bloodlines.
8522 Unique unit Chu Ko Nu.
  Unique techs Rocketry and Great Wall.
  Start with three extra villagers, minus fifty wood and two hundred food.
  Technologies cost five, ten, and fifteen percent less in Feudal, Castle, and Imperial Age.
  Town Centers support fifteen population and plus seven line of sight.
  Fire ships move faster in Castle and Imperial Age.
  Team bonus: Farms contain ten percent more food.
8523 Unique unit Mangudai.
  Unique techs Drill and Nomads.
  Hunters work faster.
  Scout cavalry line plus twenty then thirty percent hit points in Castle and Imperial Age.
  Cavalry archers fire faster.
  Build pastures instead of farms; no mill.
  Team bonus: Scout cavalry line plus two line of sight.
8524 Unique unit Cataphract.
  Unique techs Logistica and Greek Fire.
  Buildings gain extra hit points each age.
  Imperial Age costs thirty-three percent less.
  Camels, skirmishers, and spearmen cost less.
  Fire ships attack faster.
  Town Watch and Town Patrol are free.
  Team bonus: Monks heal faster.
8525 Unique unit Samurai.
  Unique techs Yasama and Kataparuto.
  Infantry attack faster from the Feudal Age.
  Fishing ships have more hit points and work faster.
  Mills and camps cost less.
  Team bonus: Galleys plus four line of sight.
8526 Unique unit Teutonic Knight.
  Unique techs Ironclad and Crenellations.
  Barracks and stable units plus melee armor in Castle and Imperial Age.
  Farms cost less wood.
  Murder Holes and Herbal Medicine are free.
  Team bonus: resist conversion.
8527 Unique unit Berserk.
  Unique techs Chieftains and Bogsveigar.
  Infantry hit points plus twenty percent from Feudal Age.
  Wheelbarrow and Hand Cart are free.
  Warships cost less.
  Docks can train Longboats.
  Team bonus: Docks cost less.
8528 Unique unit Rattan Archer.
  Unique techs Chatras and Paper Money.
  Archery Range units plus twenty percent hit points.
  Reveal enemy Town Centers at the start.
  Conscription is free.
  Economic technologies cost no wood and research faster.
  Team bonus: Imperial Skirmisher upgrade is available.
8529 Unique unit Organ Gun.
  Unique techs Circumnavigation and Arquebus.
  Ships plus ten percent hit points.
  All units cost twenty percent less gold.
  Foragers also gather wood.
  Can build a Feitoria.
  Team bonus: technologies research faster.
8530 Unique unit Jaguar Warrior.
  Unique techs Atlatl and Garland Wars.
  Start with fifty extra gold.
  Villagers carry three more.
  Military trains faster.
  Monks gain five hit points per monastery technology.
  Team bonus: relics generate extra gold.
  No stables or cavalry.
8531 Unique unit Woad Raider.
  Unique techs Stronghold and Furor Celtica.
  Infantry speed plus five percent each age from Dark Age.
  Lumberjacks work faster.
  Siege weapons fire faster.
  Can steal enemy flock even if a unit stands next to it.
  Team bonus: Siege Workshops work faster.
8532 Unique unit Gbeto.
  Unique techs Tigui and Farimba.
  Buildings cost fifteen percent less wood except farms.
  Barracks units gain pierce armor by age.
  Villagers drop off ten percent more gold.
  Team bonus: Universities work faster.
