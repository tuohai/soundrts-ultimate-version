; 2 — The Maid of Orleans (AoE2 DE Joan of Arc, ch. 2)
;
; Faithful rewrite of AoE2 DE ch. 2 "The Maid of Orleans".  Source
; walkthroughs (ageofempires.fandom.com/wiki/The_Maid_of_Orleans,
; standardof.net, aok.heavengames.com) describe this chapter as a
; long, two-phase mission: first a small escort reaches Blois to
; pick up the French army + 6 Trade Carts, then Joan + the carts
; are ferried across the Loire by 2 Transport Ships to the city of
; Orleans, where Orleans hands over its town + villagers to the
; player.  After that the player builds up to Castle Age (population
; cap 125, Imperial forbidden) and must destroy 1 of the 2 English
; Castles.  Joan and the cathedral in Orleans must survive.
;
; Map grid (12x8):
;   [a1..a8] is the south-west corner (Chinon, starting position);
;   column e is the Blois camp (large neutral French army, trade carts);
;   column f is the south English keep (just across the river from Blois);
;   row 4 is the Loire (terrain river; the bridge is at f4, with a
;   Burgundian guard tower on the south side at f3);
;   column h is Orleans (town_center, cathedral, several allied French
;   militia/archer defenders) — handed to the player on completion of
;   objective 3;
;   column k is the north English keep (the Castle the player must
;   destroy to complete objective 4);
;   column l is the outer English fortification (the other Castle, kept
;   to make the map feel like a siege ring).

; --- Vaucouleurs / Chinon (player start, a1) -----------------------------
; Faithful to AoE2 DE ch. 2 starting force:
;   - 1 Joan of Arc (npc_joan, hero unit, MUST survive)
;   - 1 Duke d'Alençon (npc_d_alencon, second hero, can fight)
;   - 1 Scout Cavalry (aoe_scout / scout_cavalry)
;   - 2 Two-Handed Swordsmen (man_at_arms upgrade of militia)
;   - 4 Knights (aoe_knight)
;   - 6 Trade Carts (trade_cart) -- the supply train to Orleans
;   - 50F 200W to bootstrap, but most resources come from Blois+Orleans

; combat hero who can fight but is not a key unit.

; The trailing "-imperial_age" forbids the Imperial Age research

; --- Blois (e2+e3) --------------------------------------------------------
; The French army + trade carts await Joan here.  When Joan reaches Blois
; the (add_units) trigger below moves the army to the player.  The camp
; itself stays as Gaia decoration so the minimap shows a French base.
; IMPORTANT: do NOT pack all of these into a single 8x8 cell.
; soundrts' square_capacity is the map's ``square_width`` (12) expressed in
; PRECISION, so 24+ units in e2 saturate the cell and the engine's
; have_enough_square_space check then blocks the army from walking to
; the exits (see CHANGELOG_AUDIT / soundrts/worldroom.py).  Split the
; camp across e2+e3 (the decoration), and the (add_units) trigger
; below spawns the reinforcements into e1+d2 (sibling cells with room
; to maneuver out of Blois toward d3 / d4 / c2).

computer_only 0 0 0 0 neutral faction franks e3 4 trade_cart 8 aoe_knight 12 aoe_archer
computer_only 0 0 0 0 neutral faction franks e3 monastery 2 monk

; --- Objective 1: Joan reaches Blois (e2/e3) ------------------------------
; When Joan reaches the Blois camp, Blois hands over its full French
; army to the player.  Cut scene plays the DE dialogue ("the French
; army is yours to command").  Mirrors ch. 1 Baudricourt escort trigger
; pattern.  npc_d_alencon is a combat hero who can fight.
; Faithful to AoE2 DE ch. 2 starting force: when Joan reaches e2/e3
; the reinforcements spawn across e2 + e1 + d2 (24 units in one
; 8x8 cell would saturate the cell and the engine's
; have_enough_square_space check would block the army from walking
; to the exits — see CHANGELOG_AUDIT / soundrts/worldroom.py
; square_capacity).  Splitting into 3 neighbouring cells gives the
; units room to maneuver out of Blois.
trigger player1 (or (has_entered e2 npc_joan) (has_entered e3 npc_joan)) (do (cut_scene 8835) (add_units e2 4 aoe_knight 6 aoe_archer 2 trade_cart e1 2 aoe_knight 3 aoe_archer 1 trade_cart d2 2 aoe_knight 3 aoe_archer 1 trade_cart))
trigger player1 (has_entered e2 npc_joan) (objective_complete 1)
trigger player1 (has_entered e3 npc_joan) (objective_complete 1)

; --- Objective 2: 6 trade_carts reach Orleans TC (h6) ---------------------
; Hand over the Orleans camp to the player when Joan + 6 trade_carts
; arrive.  Cut scene plays the DE dialogue ("the supplies are here; the
; city of Orleans is yours").  transfer_units moves the Orleans
; militia/archer/etc onto the player so they can defend the city.
trigger player1 (and (has_entered h6 npc_joan) (has 6 trade_cart) (has_entered h6 trade_cart)) (do (cut_scene 8836) (transfer_units computer4 player1) (transfer_units computer5 player1) (objective_complete 2))

; --- Objective 3: Reach Castle Age ----------------------------------------
; Once the player has dark_age + feudal_age + castle_age in upgrades,
; objective 3 is complete.  Imperial Age is FORBIDDEN via the "-imperial_age"
; entry on the player line above (so the chapter is capped at Castle Age,
; population 125, matching AoE2 DE ch. 2).
trigger player1 (and (has castle_age) (not (has imperial_age))) (objective_complete 3)

; --- Objective 4: Destroy 1 English Castle -------------------------------
; AoE2 DE ch. 2: "Convince the British to withdraw by destroying 1 of
; their Castles."  Use enemy_building_destroyed on k5 OR l5 (any of the
; two English Castles counts).  When either is gone, the chapter's main
; victory condition is met -- play the victory cut_scene.
trigger player1 (or (enemy_building_destroyed k5 aoe_castle) (enemy_building_destroyed l5 aoe_castle)) (do (cut_scene 8838) (objective_complete 4))

; --- Defeat conditions ----------------------------------------------------
; Joan must survive (key unit).  The cathedral in Orleans (npc_cathedral)
; must survive (DE ch. 2 explicit victory condition).  All 6 trade_carts
; must survive (DE ch. 2 explicit; trade_cart death is a soft fail).
trigger player1 (key_unit_killed npc_joan) (defeat)
trigger player1 (key_unit_killed npc_cathedral) (defeat)
trigger player1 (no_unit_left) (defeat)

; Auto-defeat computers whose last unit dies (so the campaign map's
; "Joan victorious" hero portrait doesn't get stuck on Blois/Orleans).
trigger all (no_unit_left) (defeat)

; Trade-cart protection: by timer 600 the player MUST still have 6
; trade_carts alive.  If even one is lost, the supply run to Orleans
; has failed — match the DE ch. 2 hard fail "deliver 6 trade carts".
trigger player1 (and (timer 600) (lt (count trade_cart) 6)) (defeat)
