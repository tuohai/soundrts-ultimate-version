; Goal 1: Deliver all three relics to the Reims cathedral at c5.
; The three relics use the AoE2 mod's ``relic`` item id (see the
; placement lines above).  ``(npc_has_items ...)`` is the *counting*
; variant of ``(npc_has_item ...)``: the latter asks "does this unit
; hold this item TYPE at all" (received_items is a set, so one relic
; was enough and the goal completed on the first delivery).
;
; ``(npc_has_items npc_reims_cathedral relic 3 c5)`` requires the
; cathedral to have received THREE relics: the count comes from
; ``received_item_counts`` (incremented by the ``give`` action) and
; from the cathedral's current inventory, whichever is larger.  So the
; goal only completes once all three are actually delivered -- the
; player may deliver them one at a time or all at once, but two relics
; are not enough.
