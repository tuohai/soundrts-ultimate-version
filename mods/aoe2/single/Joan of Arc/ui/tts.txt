8800 Joan of Arc
8801 France, 1429. Follow Joan of Arc as she lifts the siege of Orleans, marches on Reims for the coronation, and drives the English from the Loire. Five short chapters.

; Intros
8810 March Joan east from Vaucouleurs (a1) past the English patrols to Robert de Baudricourt at b4, who will grant her a holy letter and a small escort. Then carry the letter to the Dauphin's court at Chinon (f4). Joan must survive.
8811 Orleans is besieged by an English ring of watch towers and a small fort. Train militia to rally the townsfolk, then break the siege and destroy every English tower.
8812 After Orleans, Joan leads the army across the Loire. Push east from the French base (d4), capture St. Loup (a1) on the south bank and Les Tourelles (f5) on the north bank to break the English siege.
8813 Escort the Dauphin through Burgundy to Reims (c5) for the coronation. Carry three relics to the cathedral. The Burgundians and English will try to stop you.
8814 Drive Joan up the Loire and destroy every English outpost. Once every tower and castle is gone, Joan carries it home.
8815 Hold Compiègne (b4) against the combined English and Burgundian assault until Charles VII is crowned at Reims. Then escort Joan across the bridge to safety.
