; Intros
8610 Move your militia east to the Scottish village. Select units, then order them to the flagged square.
8611 Gather food, wood, and gold. Build farms if you need more food. Reach 50 of each.
8612 Build a barracks if you need one, then train four militia.
8613 Advance to the Feudal Age and research Man-at-Arms at the barracks.
8614 Defeat the English army and destroy their watch tower.
8615 Capture three relics with monks and bring them to your monastery.
8616 Build a castle, then destroy the English castle.
