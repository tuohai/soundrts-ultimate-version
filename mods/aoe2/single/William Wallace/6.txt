; Castle Age Scots with monastery + monk; bring relics to a1 monastery.
;
; The MARKET is the AoE2 DE trade/tribute hub: it unlocks the market UI
; (buy / sell / pay tribute to your allies) and it is the drop-off point
; for the trade carts.  ``market`` requires a ``mill`` in the AoE2 tech
; tree, so the mill comes with it.  Without a market the player had no
; way at all to trade or to pay tribute -- the engine's whole market /
; tribute system was unreachable in this chapter.
;
; The trade cart is the player's income: a trade unit that hauls gold
; back to the market, so the player can afford the militia the chapter
; needs.
player 500 500 500 500 a1 town_center monastery mill market 1 trade_cart 3 monk 4 house barracks 4 peasant 6 militia castle_age

; ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
; THE SCOTTISH ALLY PAYS THE PLAYER FOR THE RELICS.
;
; This is the AoE2 DE "Forge an Alliance" reward: the Scottish ally at
; a4 hands the player gold for every relic delivered to the monastery.
; The engine's tribute action moves the resources between two players,
; so the ally's gold is what actually arrives in the player's purse.
;
; The amount is 200 gold per relic (3 relics = 600 gold), which is the
; DE's relief for a chapter where the player has no market income.
;
; Three ONE-SHOT triggers with growing counts are used instead of one
; repeating trigger: a ``(has_brought_item a1 relic 1)`` condition stays
; true forever once the first relic is delivered, so a ``repeat``
; trigger would pay the player over and over.  Each count trigger
; fires exactly once, when that many relics have arrived.
; ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
trigger player1 (has_brought_item a1 relic 1) (tribute from computer1 gold 200 player1)
trigger player1 (has_brought_item a1 relic 2) (tribute from computer1 gold 200 player1)
trigger player1 (has_brought_item a1 relic 3) (tribute from computer1 gold 200 player1)
