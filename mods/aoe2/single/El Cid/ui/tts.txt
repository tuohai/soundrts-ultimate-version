; Chapter intros (the ``intro`` line of each 1.txt - 6.txt).
9170 Year 1072. El Cid comes to the tournament of King Sancho II to fight for his sword and his name. But Sancho's brother Alfonso claims Castile too, and the Cid's sword alone cannot settle the matter.
9171 Year 1081. The Cid is the greatest captain in Castile and the greatest prize of the war. Toledo has risen against the king, and only the Imam can quiet it. The Cid must first buy the Imam's help with four holy relics.
9172 Year 1081. Castile has driven the Cid into exile. He crosses the border with nothing but his sword, and takes service with Motamid of Zaragoza — but the road east is watched by Alfonso's watchtowers, and Count Berenguer waits beyond it.
9173 Year 1086. Yusuf ibn Tashfin has crossed the strait, and the Black Guards hold the coast. The Cid must rescue King Alfonso from the field and walk him home, while Yusuf's fleets and his mosque on the shore weigh on the balance.
9174 Year 1087. The Cid is a wanderer again, and Denia is the last town he can hold in the north. When the Frankish host of Count Berenguer drives him out, the only road left runs south, over the Ebro, to Valencia — where the Valencians have already begun the Wonder.
9175 Year 1094. The Cid is dead — but his body stands propped on Babieca before the castle of Valencia, and the garrison does not know it. Three armies come for the city: Berenguer's Black Guard in the north, the Black Guard fleet on the coast, and Yusuf ibn Tashfin across the sea. Ximena Díaz holds what is left.
