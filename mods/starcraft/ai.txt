; StarCraft computer scripts. Names are the mod's own types (scv / marine,
; probe / zealot, drone / zergling), not the base-pack peasant / footman / …
; ``clear`` drops res/ai.txt so beginner/nightmare cannot fall back to Warcraft.

clear

; Terran
; ===========================================================================

def sc_terran_easy
defeat_score 10
counter_skill 25
watchdog 900
workers 8
label sc_terran_easy
get 6 scv
get 1 supply_depot
get 1 barracks
get 8 scv 6 marine
get 1 refinery
get 10 scv 10 marine
goto sc_terran_easy

def sc_terran_aggressive
defeat_score 20
